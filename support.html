Common questions

My workouts aren’t on my other device.

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Pesado (Settings › [your name] › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

My workouts aren’t showing up in Apple Health.

In Pesado, go to Profile and turn on Save to Apple Health. If Pesado says it doesn’t have permission, allow it in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pesado. Only workouts finished after turning it on are saved.

Why don’t I see calories?

Calories are estimated from your workout time and the body weight saved in the Health app. Add your weight in Health and allow Pesado to read it.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Open Profile, tap Become a Pesado Subscriber, then Restore Subscription. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Pesado, open Profile and tap Manage next to Pesado Subscriber, or go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. You keep subscriber features until the end of the period you paid for.
