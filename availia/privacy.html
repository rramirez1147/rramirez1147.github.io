AVAILIA

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Availia helps groups find a time to meet. It’s made by Ricardo Ramirez. This policy explains what Availia stores, who can see it, and how long it’s kept. The short version: Availia only stores what it needs to schedule, nobody sees your free times or calendar, and scheduling details are deleted soon after an event ends.

Your account

  • You sign in with Apple. Availia receives your name (the first time only) and an email address, which can be a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email.
  • Availia stores your name and time zone so friends see who you are and times appear correctly.
  • Friends who answer from an invitation link don’t create an account. They type a name, and their browser gets a temporary guest sign-in that’s removed once their events are cleaned up.

Events and groups

  • When you create an event, Availia stores its name, notes, location, dates, preferred days and times, and the names of the people you invite.
  • Groups store their name and members.
  • People in an event can see its details and who’s invited. People in a group can see its members.

Your availability and calendar

  • The times you mark as free are stored for that event. Only you can see them. Availia’s scheduling engine reads everyone’s times privately and shares only the times that work best and how many people can make each one.
  • If you connect Apple Calendar, Availia reads the calendars you choose on your iPhone and sends only the start and end of times you’re busy. Event names, notes, locations, and attendees never leave your device. Events marked Free are ignored.
  • You can turn off calendars in Profile › Calendars, or remove access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars.

Votes

If a group votes on a time, everyone sees the totals. Only the organizer sees who voted for what.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Availia stores your device’s push token to send updates about your events, delivered through Apple. You can turn reminders off in Profile › Notifications, or all notifications in Settings.

How long things are kept

  • 14 days after an event ends, everyone’s availability, the invitation links, the votes, and the times that weren’t chosen are deleted automatically. The event’s name, who was invited, and the final time are kept so your group can see past events.
  • Guest sign-ins are removed once none of their events need them.
  • Notifications are deleted after 90 days.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it.

Deleting your data

Delete your account anytime in Profile › Delete Account. This removes your account, your availability and votes, the groups you own, and the events you organize. On other people’s events your name stays on the guest list. Guests who answered from a link can email to have their responses removed.

Who processes data

  • Supabase hosts Availia’s database in the United States.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and delivers notifications.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the invitation pages.

What Availia doesn’t do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No access to your address book. If you pick people with Apple’s contact picker, Availia only receives those people, and their phone number or email stays on your iPhone. It’s used only to fill in the invitation text or email, and is never uploaded.

Children

Availia isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
