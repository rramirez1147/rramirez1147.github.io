Help & Support

How does Availia pick a time?

Everyone shares when they’re free, by hand or from their calendar. Availia finds the time the most people can make, favoring your preferred days and hours and sooner dates. If one time is clearly best, it’s recommended right away. If a few are equally good, the group votes. The organizer always confirms the final time.

Do my friends need the app?

No. Each person gets their own link. They open it in their browser, type their name, and pick the days and times they’re free. Groups are for people with the app.

Can friends see my calendar?

No. Nobody sees your calendar or your free times. Availia only shares the times that work best and how many people can make each.

Someone lost their link

Open the event, then tap the share button next to their name to send it again. If they open it on a new phone, their earlier answers come with it.

I’m not getting notifications

Check Profile › Notifications in Availia, and Settings › Notifications › Availia on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile › Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy for what’s removed and what’s kept.
