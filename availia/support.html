Help & Support

How does Eventos pick a time?

Everyone adds the times they’re free. Eventos finds the time the most people can make, favoring your preferred days and hours and sooner dates. After the answer-by time, it plans with whoever answered. With Set the Time for Me on, the best time is set and everyone’s told. Otherwise, or if a few times are equally good, the host picks one.

Do my friends need the app?

No. Each person gets their own link. They open it in their browser, type their name, and pick the days and times they’re free. Groups are for people with the app.

Can friends see when I’m free?

No. Nobody sees your free times, and Eventos never reads your calendar. It only shares the times that work best and how many people can make each.

Someone lost their link

Open the event, then tap the share button next to their name to send it again. If they open it on a new phone, their earlier answers come with it.

I’m not getting notifications

Check Profile › Notifications in Eventos, and Settings › Notifications › Eventos on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile › Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy for what’s removed and what’s kept.
