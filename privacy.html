PESADO

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Pesado is a workout tracker made by Ricardo Ramirez. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Pesado doesn’t collect your data. Your workouts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account, and the developer can’t see them.

What Pesado stores, and where

  • Workouts and templates are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices. They’re protected by your Apple Account. The developer has no access to them.
  • Settings such as your name, profile photo, weight unit, and accent color are stored on your device and synced through iCloud in the same way.
  • If you turn iCloud off for Pesado, everything stays on your device only.

Apple Health

If you turn on “Save to Apple Health”:

  • Pesado writes your finished workouts and an estimate of the active calories you burned to the Health app.
  • Pesado reads only your most recent body weight, to calculate that estimate. It’s used on your device at that moment and isn’t stored or sent anywhere.
  • Health information is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

You can change or remove this access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pesado.

Camera and photos

Pesado uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to: to set a profile photo, or to put your stats on a photo you share. Share images are saved to your photo library only when you tap Save. None of this is uploaded anywhere by Pesado.

Sharing

When you share a workout image, it goes only where you send it, such as Messages or Instagram Stories. Once shared, that app’s privacy policy applies.

Subscriptions

Pesado Subscriber is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and Pesado never receives your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. Pesado checks with the App Store only whether a subscription is active.

What Pesado doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Children

Pesado isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete workouts in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Pesado.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
